Using this browser

  • Use it like a normal browser. Click, type, scroll, copy and paste text and images, manage tabs, and watch videos (with sound) — it all works.
  • You share each browser with agents, through a queue. Only one of you drives at a time; whoever else wants it waits in line. Click “Return control to agents” to hand it back when you're done.
  • An agent may hand a browser to you for something it can't do itself — a captcha or a login check. Control comes to you and this bar turns amber; finish the step, then return control.
  • Logins are saved. Each browser keeps its own cookies and sign-ins, and they persist across restarts — so you stay logged in.
  • Keyboard shortcuts mostly pass through to the browser (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+F, Ctrl+L, Ctrl+R, copy/paste, …). Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+N and Ctrl+Tab control your OWN browser's tabs and can't be captured here — use Fullscreen to send those too. On a Mac, use Ctrl (not Cmd) for these.
