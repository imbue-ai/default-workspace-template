`keybindings.ts` gains `chordDisplayLabel`, which writes a canonical chord the way a menu does ("⌘⇧N" on an Apple platform, "Ctrl+Shift+N" elsewhere), `chordKeycapLabels`, the separate keys it is built from, `chordSpokenLabel`, which names a chord in words ("Command+Shift+N") for tooltips and screen readers, and `isApplePlatform`, the platform check the shell's launcher already used.

`embed.ts` exports two embed contract v15 types, probed like the other newer ones: `KEYBOARD_SHORTCUTS_PAGE` (`minds:keyboard-shortcuts-page`), with which the shell's keyboard shortcuts page reports its height and whether its recorder is listening, and `OPEN_KEYBOARD_SHORTCUTS` (`minds:open-keyboard-shortcuts`), with which the shell asks Imbue Studio to open its Workspace settings at the Keyboard shortcuts group. `EMBEDDER_CAPABILITIES` documents the new optional `opensKeyboardShortcuts` field.
