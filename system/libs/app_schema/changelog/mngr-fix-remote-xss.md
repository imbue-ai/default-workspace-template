`app_schema.request_guard.install_request_guard(app, exempt_rules)`: the Flask hook every built-in app installs ahead of its routes, refusing with a 403 `ErrorResponse` any request the provenance check of `workspace_layout.identity` does not admit (a page of another origin, or a request with no `X-Imbue-Identity` header from anywhere but loopback under a loopback `Host`), skipping the rules named in `exempt_rules`, and answering a present identity header that does not parse with a 400 on every route. The library now depends on `workspace-layout`.
