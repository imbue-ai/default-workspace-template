`app.toml` gains an optional `[preview]` table (`PreviewSpec`): how a throwaway instance of the app boots for a preview. `command` (default: the app's program as its console script), `ports` (named free ports; `main` is always one), `env` and `args` (which may carry `{port:<name>}`, `{copy:<key>}`, `{host}`, `{scratch}`, `{registry}`, and `{shell_url}`), `copies` (repo-relative directories copied into the instance's scratch space, by key), `health_path` (default `/health`), `open_path` (default `/`), and `open_path_takes_key` (whether `open_path` carries `{key}`). A placeholder that names an undeclared port or copy, an unknown placeholder, or an `open_path` whose `{key}` disagrees with `open_path_takes_key` fails validation. A manifest without the table gets `scaffold_preview_spec(name)`, the build-app convention (`<PACKAGE_UPPER>_PORT`, `_HOST`, and `_DATA_DIR` over a copy of `data/.apps/<name>`), so every app previews by construction.
