A manifest may declare `launcher_rank` (an integer, at least 1): where the app's tile sits on the New Tab page's leading "Open new" row, lowest first; an app without one follows on the second row. Registry rows carry `launcher_rank` and, on each action, `params` (the names of the manifest's params), both read as absent when missing. `forward_port.py` copies both, and its registry writer now emits integers and arrays of strings inside inline tables.
