`app_manifest.content_security_policy` is new: `attach_document_policy(app_or_blueprint, overrides=None)` gives every HTML page an app serves a `Content-Security-Policy` (same-origin scripts plus the page's own inline `<script>` blocks admitted by hash, inline styles, images from the page's own origin plus `data:` and `blob:`, frames of any http(s) page, same-origin fetches and sockets, no plugins, no `<base>` retargeting, no cross-origin form posts), leaving a response that sets its own policy alone. The workspace's Flask apps (the shell, the chat, the terminal, Getting Started, the browser) attach it; `app_manifest.testing.assert_document_policy_admits_page` checks a served page against its header, `url_origin(url)` gives the source for one origin an app opens a directive to, and `inline_source(text)` gives the `'sha256-...'` source for one inline script or stylesheet (the shell's parked-port page uses it for its stylesheet). The library now depends on Flask and Werkzeug.
