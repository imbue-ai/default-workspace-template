A launch path may declare `text_param`, naming one of its `params` as the parameter the launcher fills with typed text (launcher-and-getting-started plan section 3.1); naming a param the launch path does not declare fails to load with an `InvalidManifestValueError`. `LaunchParamName` is the primitive for a launch param's name, on `LaunchParam.name`, `LaunchPath.text_param`, and the registry row's `RegistryLaunchPath.params` and `text_param`.
