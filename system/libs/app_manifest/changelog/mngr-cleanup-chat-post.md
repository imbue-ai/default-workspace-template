A launch path may declare `method = "POST"` (default `GET`), `presets` (fixed string name-value pairs the shell sends with every launch), and `draft_param` (one of its params, the one filled with text to be drafted rather than sent; at most one of `text_param` and `draft_param`). Preset names may not repeat a param's, and the reserved envelope names `client_id`, `desktop_id`, and `window_path` are refused for both. The registry's launch path rows carry the three fields, reading old rows as GETs with no presets.
