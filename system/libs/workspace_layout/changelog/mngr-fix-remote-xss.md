`workspace_layout.identity` gains the request provenance check every in-workspace service installs ahead of its routes (`describe_provenance_refusal` over a `RequestProvenance`, with `is_loopback_address`, `is_loopback_host` and `is_from_another_origin`): a request a browser marks as a page of another origin's (`Sec-Fetch-Site`, other than a GET or HEAD navigation) is refused whatever it carries, a request a proxy stamped is read by its `X-Imbue-Identity` header, and a header-less request is admitted from loopback alone, under a loopback `Host`. The workspace's own browser (the Screen's Chromium) is on loopback too, so a page it shows could otherwise have reached every service as the owner (imbue-ai/mngr-internal#2816).
