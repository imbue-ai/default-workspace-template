`presentation.py` gains three pure functions about a routine an app owns, read from the directory its definition file is in rather than from the definition's `app` field: `is_schedule_only` (only its schedule and its switch can be changed), `has_schedule_override` (the user's schedule is in place of the app's) and `app_schedule_text` (the app's own schedule in words meanwhile). The Routines app adds them to each row it answers. The rows `routines list` and `routines show` print are unchanged.
