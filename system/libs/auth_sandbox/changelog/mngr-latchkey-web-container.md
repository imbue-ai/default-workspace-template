- New library for the web latchkey auth sandbox: the commands owner-exec runs on the Imbue Studio web chrome's behalf (`auth-sandbox-sign-in`, `auth-sandbox-set-credentials`, `auth-sandbox-import-browser-state`, `auth-sandbox-export`), thin click entry points over `imbue.mngr_latchkey.core.Latchkey` (the desktop's sign-in sequence) and `latchkey auth re-encrypt`, with keys read from `/run/auth-sandbox/` and passed on stdin or in the environment, never in an argument. They exit 0 when done, 1 when latchkey refused (its reason on stderr) and 3 when a material they need is missing or malformed, the `latchkey` binary and its config included; 2 stays click's usage-error code.

- The sandbox's boot script (`sandbox/boot.sh`, installed at `/opt/auth-sandbox/boot.sh`) and supervisord configuration (the Screen service with its stack group and watcher, and owner-exec; nothing else), and the image build steps `system/scripts/build_auth_sandbox_image.py` runs in a Modal sandbox before snapshotting it (every build-time `latchkey` invocation runs under a throwaway directory and key, since the CLI refuses to start without one and a sandbox has no keychain to mint one from); the build fails when the image's latchkey is not the version `setup_system.sh` pins, or is below the floor the venv's `imbue-mngr-latchkey` enforces, rather than producing an image whose every sign-in refuses to start).

- The template `config.json` the image carries: minds' hidden built-ins and registered services plus a browser entry naming the Fortress launcher, rendered by `auth-sandbox-render-config` at image build and verified with `latchkey ensure-browser --source existing-config`.

- The sandbox is reached over Modal's own TLS (`encrypted_ports` for the Screen on 8090 and owner-exec on 8793, `unencrypted_ports` for neko's ICE TCP mux on 59000), with no share gateway, relay or certificate in it. The connector passes everything at create, with no secrets: the boot script requires `AUTH_SANDBOX_AUTHORIZED_KEY` (written as the whole of `authorized_keys`), `AUTH_SANDBOX_CHROME_ORIGIN` (written to `share.env` as owner-exec's CORS origin and passed to the Screen as its `frame-ancestors`) and `AUTH_SANDBOX_SCREEN_TOKEN_SHA256` (which turns on the Screen's owner gate), refuses a malformed one, and has the Screen and owner-exec listen on every interface. The Screen reads the media tunnel's address from `/run/auth-sandbox-connect-info.json`, which the connector writes.
