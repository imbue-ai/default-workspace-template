Every desktop in the workspace shell is shared, and a signed-in visitor arriving for the first time gets a desktop of their own, seeded from the first one (the system interface's entry has the details). The desktop-interface blueprint documents (`docs/system/blueprint/desktop-interface/`) drop the sharing mode, add the arrival route, the `users.json` file, and the `user_id` on client records, and describe the per-user desktop in a new plan section 3.10; they also list the presence routes, the `presence_updated` push, and the Presence tray widget from the merged sharing identity cleanup. The branch also carries the desktop-interface work (`mngr-desktop-ui-*.md`) and, in the share gateway, the sharing identity cleanup (`mngr-sharing-cleanup.md`).
