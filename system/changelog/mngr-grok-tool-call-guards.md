grok agents are held to the workspace's tool-call policies. `.grok/hooks/policy.json` runs `system/scripts/agent_grok_policy_guards.py` before each grok tool call; it hands the call to the guard scripts claude runs, in claude's order, with grok's shell tools presented as claude's `Bash`. A `use_tool` call is judged by the call inside it, and `workflow` and truncated shell calls are refused. A refusal reaches the model with the guard's whole reason, an allowed shell call gets the OOM-band and git-identity prefix, and the step nudge and the open-steps reminder ride `additionalContext`. A guard that crashes or runs too long fails open and is logged to `grok_policy_guards.log` in the agent's state dir. On `Stop`, grok runs the stop nudge and, at a turn's end only, code-guardian's stop gate.

The secret-file guard refuses grok's `read_file`, `search_replace` and `write` under `data/.secrets/` (or mcpc's store) and a `spawn_subagent` started there; the step nudge skips grok's read-only tools.
