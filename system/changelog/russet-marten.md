Shared UI: a hover bubble can now sit above its trigger, for a row in a list of
rows that raise their own bubbles. The palette gains a stronger border colour
(`border-strong`), for a field that has to read as one you type into rather than
as a panel seam.

The chrome's stroke icons are Lucide's drawings, copied into the source as path
data rather than pulled in as a package, and nothing in the tree said so.
`licenses/lucide-LICENSE` now holds Lucide's licence as they publish it (ISC for
their own drawings, MIT for the Feather ones it lists), a
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` at the repo root says which files hold the borrowed
data and what is drawn here instead, and both icon modules point at it.
