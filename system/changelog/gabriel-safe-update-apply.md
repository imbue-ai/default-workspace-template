`setup_system.sh` installs a permanent update-apply recovery cron entry (`/etc/cron.d/update-apply-recover`, every 5 minutes): a plain-python3 `update_self.py recover --if-stale` that rolls back an update apply killed hard without a container restart. It is a silent no-op in every normal state -- it acts only when the apply marker exists, its recorded process is dead, and the marker has gone a grace period without an update.
