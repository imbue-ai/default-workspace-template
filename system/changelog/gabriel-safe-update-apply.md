`setup_system.sh` installs a permanent update-apply recovery cron entry (`/etc/cron.d/update-apply-recover`, every 5 minutes): a plain-python3 `update_self.py recover --if-stale` that rolls back an update apply killed hard without a container restart. It is a silent no-op in every normal state -- it acts only when the apply marker exists, its recorded process is dead, and the marker has gone a grace period without an update. The drop-in carries its own `PATH` line, because cron does not inherit the image's `ENV PATH` and the recovery shells out to `mngr`, `uv` and `npm` to put the running state back, and takes a `flock`, because a tick that acts routinely outlasts the five minutes until the next one; it logs to `/var/log/supervisor/update-apply-recover.log` alongside the other scheduled jobs.
