- A `tk start` or `tk close` of a message's step id now tells the agent what the chat's model suggested when the message repeated, changed or called off one of its open steps: work under that step, close the old step and work under the message's own step, or stop and close the called-off step with a short note. A `tk start` of a message's own step that replaces a step still open is held until the agent closes the old one (`system/scripts/agent_message_steps.py`).

- The note beside a message sent while the agent was busy says the message may repeat, change or call off an open step, and AGENTS.md describes those cases. The chat never changes the agent's steps itself.
