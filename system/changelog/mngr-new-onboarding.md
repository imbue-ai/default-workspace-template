The desktop interface contracts (`docs/system/blueprint/desktop-interface/contracts.md`) describe the show-around tour: its record (`tour.json`, section 4.5a), the `GET` and `POST /api/tour` routes, the `minds:highlight-chrome` message the shell sends (section 5), and the tour's markers (section 12): `data-tour-card`, `data-tour-badge`, `data-tour-paused`, `data-tour-exit`, `data-tour-back`, `data-tour-next`, `data-tour-link`, and the launcher's `data-tour-row`. The pinned window's first frame is now most of the backdrop (sections 4.2 and the pinned-taskbar-entries plan), and the launcher plan and the stop-when-no-windows spec record that Getting Started's first-visit window is gone.

The shared `workspace_ui` library's `HIGHLIGHT_CHROME` export is described in its own changelog (`system/libs/workspace_ui/changelog/`).
