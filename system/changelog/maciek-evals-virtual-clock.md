The workspace image builds libfaketime (upstream master at a pinned commit, FAKE_STAT compiled out) into `/usr/local/lib/faketime/libfaketime.so.1`, from `system/scripts/install_libfaketime.sh`, which `setup_system.sh` runs on every provider. Nothing loads it by default: it is there for a workspace whose creator puts supervisord's programs on a shifted clock through `system/clock.env`, as the minds evals harness does to let days pass between two steps of a task. The installer also puts a `tmux` in `/usr/local/bin` that drops a libfaketime preload before it runs the real one, since tmux deadlocks under libfaketime and a program on the shifted clock still drives it.
