`docs/specs/chat-send-failure-system.md` covers mngr's `submission_unconfirmed`, `agent_not_found` and `not_submitted` send-failure kinds: what the chat's failure notice offers for each, why an unconfirmed send gets Send again and no Force, how the chat takes its composer copy back out once the agent's turn for it arrives, that the chat app sends a message again once on its own after `agent_not_found` or `not_submitted`, where nothing was delivered, and that Retry goes under the failed send's send-time id, with a send that got no answer handled as `submission_unconfirmed`.
