- The template catalog (`catalog/new-tab-templates.json`) gains an `apps` array for published apps, and a shelf names an app as `app:<name>` among its template slugs. A workspace from before this ignores both. `catalog/build_catalog_from_export.py` lists an app from a checkout of its published repository (`--app <repository url>=<checkout>`, `--drop-app <name>`), reading its `app.toml` and copying its icon, and no longer needs the templates export to rebuild the catalog.

- `docs/system/specs/routines/spec.md` describes the four fields a routine's row carries about the app that owns it and the Routines app's `DELETE /api/routines/<id>/schedule`; `docs/system/blueprint/new-tab-page/plan-new-tab-page.md` describes apps in the catalog (the `apps` array, the `app:<name>` shelf entry, and that an app appears only once its entry and icon are on the `mngr/new-tab-page` branch); and `seed_welcome_chat.py` says its body may carry `first_message`.

- `AGENTS.md` describes how an app is shared, taken and updated (`publish-app`, `install-app`, `update-installed-app`), the two repositories an app can have (a public published one and a private dev repository, `<name>-dev`), when `app-install link` is run, that a record is changed with `app-install reference add` and never by hand, that a skill built for an app is made under `.agents/skills/` and taken in when the app is published, and that a template shares a whole workspace.

- `system/libs/README.md` says `app_schema` is what an app built in the workspace depends on to get the workspace's app SDK, and that a published app depends on the released `imbue-app-sdk` instead.
