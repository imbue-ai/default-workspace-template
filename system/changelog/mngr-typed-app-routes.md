The workspace app model's contracts (section 2) say an app keeps a route table naming each route's models or the reason it exchanges no JSON, reads bodies through `app_schema.routes.parse_request_body` and answers through `app_schema.routes.model_response`, which fail a request the table does not describe. The desktop interface's and the app model's contracts record the shell's two changed wire shapes: `GET /api/updates/pending` answers `{"notice": notice | null}`, and a relay delivery carries `shown`, `has_no_desktop_window` and `window_id` as explicit nulls for a posted message.
