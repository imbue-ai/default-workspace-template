The shared frontend library gains the workspace's one menu, its one dropdown, and the geometry both place themselves with.

`components/menu.ts` is the Menu every menu in the workspace now is: `createMenu(options)` owns opening, closing, the invisible sheet under an open menu, Escape, placement against the trigger, and submenus, and a caller describes rows (`action`, `submenu`, `value`, `check`, `divider`, and `custom` for content that is not a plain row). The rules are the component's rather than a setting: a menu opens on a click and closes on a press outside or on Escape, never on a drift, a scroll or a resize; a row may open one submenu, which opens on hover (or keyboard focus) after a short intent delay, is protected on the pointer's way across by a safe triangle, and closes when the pointer leaves the menu-and-submenu pair; a submenu cannot open another, held by the types. Every menu portals to <body> on a new `--z-popover` layer above the modal overlays, because a popover is the most recently opened thing on screen and one opened from inside a modal has to paint over it; `--z-dropdown` goes. A Menu has `dispose()`, which its owner calls from `onremove` -- a menu still open when its owner unmounts would otherwise keep its Escape listener on the window for good, and swallow Escape for every menu after it. An action row may `keepsOpen`, for an action that reports its failure on a line under the rows.

`components/dropdown.ts` is the form control that looks like a menu and is deliberately not one: it picks one value from a list and nothing else, behind the same sheet, with the same Escape.

`flyout-position.ts` becomes `menu-position.ts`, and takes `placeMenu` out of the sidebar (where the tab menu was importing it from) beside the renamed `placeSubmenu` and `isInSafeTriangle`. `placeMenu` gains `above` and an `end` alignment, and one gap and one margin serve every menu. `placeSubmenu` lines a submenu's first row up with the row that opened it and slides the box up, by exactly as much as it takes, when holding that line would run it off the bottom of the screen; the safe triangle is what tells a pointer travelling to the submenu apart from one changing its mind.

`menuRowClass` draws a row's highlight as an inset rounded slab, 4px in from the card's edges with the 4px radius the card's own 8px corner leaves once you step 4px inward. `placeTooltip` gains `above` beside `below` and `right`, as an addition rather than a change of default.

`--shadow-overlay`, the one shadow every floating surface casts, becomes a 2px contact shadow under a halo 3px down with an 8px blur and a 2px spread, both at 8% black, in place of the 1px contact under a 12px blur with no spread: the same lift, with a firmer edge and a halo that ends sooner.
