The apt Post-Invoke capture hook installed by `system/scripts/setup_system.sh` now no-ops on a rootfs that has never completed a converge (no identity stamp at `/var/lib/minds/env-converge/rootfs-id`). On such a rootfs (an image rebuild or a restore onto a new machine) the environment record is authoritative and about to be replayed, so capturing there would clobber the record before the replay reads it -- the cause of issue #523's loss of user-installed packages. The `env-converge capture` CLI carries the same guard; the hook-level line additionally protects workspace checkouts that predate it.
