The Fortress env.d unit (`system/scripts/env.d/2000-screen.sh`) installs a second managed Chromium policy, `deny-local-network-access.json`, setting `LocalNetworkAccessBlockedForUrls` to every URL: the browser on the Screen gates a public page's requests to loopback behind a permission prompt, and the policy answers that prompt for the user, so no page the browser shows can ask to reach the workspace's loopback services (imbue-ai/mngr-internal#2816). A page on loopback reaching loopback is not a local network request and is untouched. The two policy installers share one `_install_policy_file`, and `fortress_distrust_policy_test.py` is now `fortress_policies_test.py`, covering both.
