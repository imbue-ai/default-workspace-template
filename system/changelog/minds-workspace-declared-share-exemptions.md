`forward_port.py` copies the new `system` manifest key onto the registry row. Both `contracts.md` files and the workspace app model plan document the key and the services event's `system` and `internal` fields, and say the share gateway admits a per-app grant on any app that is not internal. The stop-when-no-windows spec and the desktop-interface contract now exempt an app with a per-app grant from the automatic stop only when it is not internal.
