`docs/specs/chat-send-failure-system.md` covers mngr's `submission_unconfirmed`, `agent_not_found`, `not_submitted` and `state_unknown` send-failure kinds, and what the chat app does on its own before a failure reaches the user. It sends a message once more after `submission_unconfirmed` and `unknown` to the same place, answering a send that then lands `"resent"`, which the page shows as a "Sent again" line under the message that a second copy folds into; after `agent_not_found` by discovery; after `not_submitted` to the same place. It tries again after 1, 2 and 4 seconds after `not_ready` and `state_unknown`, and once more at once after `agent_unreachable`. The spec says what the failure notice offers when that repeat fails too, why a still-unconfirmed send gets Send again and no Force, how the chat takes its composer copy back out once the agent's turn for it arrives, and which harnesses can return an unconfirmed send at all.

mngr is pinned to the commit that adds `not_submitted` and reads Claude's screen for a send it has waited out.
