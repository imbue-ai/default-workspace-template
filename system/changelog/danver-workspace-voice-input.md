`system/libs/workspace_voice/` is a new member of the npm workspace (and the image copies its manifest): dictation for the workspace's pages, which the chat's composer now offers (see the chat's and the library's own entries). The desktop interface's and the workspace app model's contracts document the field, the `app` object, and the registry key; `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` names the speech model and the speech detector dictation downloads and ships.
