The Screen spec (`docs/system/specs/screen/spec.md`) designs a neko-streamed Linux desktop as one workspace window, with the browser as a launch path of it and the browser app deleted: packaging, the Screen service, the viewer, the control lease over neko's `locked_controls`, sizing through added RANDR modes, three video profiles, visibility and audio policy, a never-stop-for-emptiness lifecycle whose group ends together, occupancy-aware OOM bands, clipboard and files, mobile, the three fullscreen layers (a `FULLSCREEN` placement state, Studio's immersive mode, and the X session's own), the share gateway's brokering, the browser migration, the `screen` command, tests and the implementation order.

The Screen's first two implementation steps land the infrastructure around the new `system/apps/screen` package:

- The env.d unit `system/scripts/env.d/2000-screen.sh` replaces `1000-playwright-fortress.sh`: it installs the Screen's apt packages (Xorg with the dummy driver, XFCE, PulseAudio, GStreamer with x264, xdotool, wmctrl, xclip) from the pinned snapshot and the pinned neko v3.2.0 binary at `/opt/neko/bin/neko` (taken from the `ghcr.io/m1k1o/neko/base` image's one layer through the registry API, checked against pinned digests), and carries Fortress and the Chrome extensions over unchanged, `DWT_SKIP_BROWSER_UNIT` included. The offload configs and `offload_sandbox_init.sh` point at the new unit.

- `system/supervisord.conf.d/xvfb.conf` is gone: display `:99` is the Screen's Xorg from now on (the browser program keeps `DISPLAY=:99` and renders onto the Screen until its migration). New drop-ins: `screen.conf` (the service and the `screen-stack` group), `screen-xorg.conf`, `screen-pulse.conf`, `screen-session.conf`, `screen-neko.conf` (the group's members, started by the service on demand) and `screen-watch.conf` (the event listener that ends the group together).

- `system/test_app_manifests.py` pins the Screen's manifest, accepts an event listener as a declared wiring program, and treats programs an app's manifest wires as that app's rather than standalone. The Dockerfile's npm manifest layer and `system/package.json` carry the viewer's frontend; its build output is gitignored like the other frontends'.

- The Screen's launch path for its own page is `screen` rather than the spec's `open`, which the manifest schema reserves for the launch path the shell synthesizes.
