New app: Streamed Browser. A full Chromium -- its own tabs, address bar, and native menus -- streamed into a workspace pane as damage-driven H.264 (pixelflux stripes, CPU-only) over one WebSocket, with display-level input injection ported from Selkies. Built for feel on small hosts and bad networks: per-stripe credit-ack flow control bounds in-flight data across every tunnel hop (congestion yields fewer, fresher frames instead of a stale backlog), the encoder's capture rate tracks what the connection actually delivers, quality softens only when the connection's own delay inflates, keyframes recover stickily instead of looping, the mouse cursor rides out-of-band (pointer motion costs zero encoding), scrolling echoes locally at ~0ms before real frames land, and settled screens re-encode crisp. Cmd-shortcuts stay local to the Mac; hidden tabs release all held keys.
