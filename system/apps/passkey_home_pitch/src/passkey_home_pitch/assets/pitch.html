The problem

Passkeys are the right answer. The way you get them is broken.

Today you choose between convenient-but-locked-in, or free-and-owned-but-unusable. Neither is good enough.

Locked in

Apple Passwords & the cloud managers

On macOS, passkeys live in iCloud Keychain. You can't see a list of them, can't export them, can't move them off Apple. The sync that makes them convenient is also the cage.

Unusable

Raw KeePassXC

The only open-source, local-first store that supports passkeys and lets you export them. But setup is a hobbyist chore: install the app, make a DB, install the extension, pair them, find the hidden toggle, hope your site isn't one of the broken ones.

How it works

Five minutes to your first passkey. A list that's actually a list. Backups that don't leak.

01 · Onboarding

Guided setup

Step-by-step screens take you from "just installed" to a real passkey on a real site in five minutes — install the browser extension (deep-linked), pair it with the app, enable passkeys, then create your first one. Hand-held.

02 · Manage

A real passkey manager

One screen showing every passkey you own — rename, delete, re-export, and a per-site "test this passkey" link. KeePassXC buries this behind a menu. We make it the home screen.

03 · Backup

Migration & backup, done right

First-class export/import. And we fix KeePassXC's one real wart — its .passkey exports are unencrypted JSON. We wrap exports in an encrypted envelope so your backup isn't a liability.
