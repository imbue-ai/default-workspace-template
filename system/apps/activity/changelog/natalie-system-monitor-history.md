System Monitor shows memory over time: a chart of the last hour, 24 hours or 7 days, showing the typical use and the range in each period, the point where closing starts, and a mark wherever something was closed to free memory. Hovering shows a period's figures, and the readings are also available as a table. Below it, "Recently closed" lists the newest closures of the last week in plain words, how much each freed, and what to do next (a closed chat picks up when messaged).

A reading is taken once a minute by a short built-in cron job (`/etc/cron.d/activity-memory-history`, written by the app when it starts, never into your own scheduled tasks), so recording holds no memory between readings; a week of readings is kept in `data/.state/activity/memory-history.tsv`. Closures come from the memory guard's own shed ledger.
