Getting Started's routes are typed: `GET /api/health` answers a `HealthResponse` (`status`, `is_frontend_built`) and `GET /api/templates-catalog` a `TemplatesCatalogResponse` whose `catalog` is a `CatalogDocument` of `TemplateEntry` rows (each template's listing with its drawing resolved to a `thumbnail_url`) and `CatalogShelf` rows, or null when no catalog is configured; its 503 is the `{"detail": ...}` error shape. The page, the bundle's assets and the app contract module are declared schemaless. The API document at `/api/schema` publishes the models, and every answer is checked against the route table.
