A stopped chat no longer keeps its transcript in memory for good after it is read. Reading a stopped chat after its stop rebuilds the chat's watcher: a thread, file watches, and the parsed transcript. Eviction fired only on the chat's transition into stopped, and no second transition comes, so every stopped chat opened since the app started stayed resident. On one production workspace, 11 claude watchers were resident for 4 live claude chats, and chat-app held 558 MiB after three days. After every observe event, chat-app now drops the resident transcript of each stopped chat that has no open stream. A chat someone is viewing keeps its transcript until the stream closes.
