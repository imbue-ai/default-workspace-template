An agent's message, or a seeded turn such as the welcome, can now offer one-click replies: a markdown link to `#reply=<text>` (for example `[Yes](#reply=yes) [No thanks](#reply=no%20thanks)`) shows as a small button, and clicking it sends `<text>` as your next message, as if you had typed it and pressed send, without touching what you were typing. A chat waiting for its first message is launched by the click. A message's buttons are disabled once you reply to it, typed or clicked. Older chat apps show these links as plain text.
