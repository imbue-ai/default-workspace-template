When a Claude agent ends its turn outside the workspace folder, the Stop hook that sends it back and the `cd` it answers with now show as a single "Back to the workspace folder" chip after the reply. Its panel says why it happened and shows the command. The hook used to break the timeline like any other Stop hook, so a step the agent had left open for the next turn was drawn a second time under the hook, holding nothing but the `cd`. Now the hook no longer breaks the turn: the open step shows once, the reply stays the reply, and the step stops spinning as soon as the hook fires. If the agent answers with anything more than a lone `cd`, the extra content shows as usual in the same turn.
