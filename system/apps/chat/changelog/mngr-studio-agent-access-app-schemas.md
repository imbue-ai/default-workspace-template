The chat app describes its own API: `GET /api/schema` answers an OpenAPI document built from its routes, every route's view carries a docstring the document shows as the operation's summary, and its manifest carries a one-line `description` and the `api_schema_path`, which the visiting-agent primer links. The document publishes the request and response schemas of every route that validates a body with a pydantic model or answers one (the chat and agent listings, create, seed, intake, send, model choice, rename, fast mode, autocompact, settings, uploads, interrupt, the queue actions, handoff, destroy, stop and start, the model and credit routes, the sign-in flow's start, poll and submit, and the latchkey scope lookup), and the request model of the focus route, whose answer is the shell's; the handoff and its retry are documented as the 202 they answer.
