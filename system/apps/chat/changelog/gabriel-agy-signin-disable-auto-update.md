Signing in to Google (Antigravity) no longer upgrades the workspace's agy. Unless told not to, agy starts a background updater on a regular run, including the bare `agy` the sign-in drives and even a signed-out `agy models`, and that updater replaces the binary it ran from with Google's latest release. The sign-in and the `agy models` check after it ran with the updater on, so one sign-in swapped the image's pinned `/usr/local/bin/agy` out from under every Antigravity chat after it. Both now run with `AGY_CLI_DISABLE_AUTO_UPDATE=true`, the same switch mngr sets for Antigravity agents.
