The composer's mic says when the speech model has to be loaded, and shows the load, in place of the 2 px progress ring users missed. The model's load and dictation are now two things, and the mic shows one of six states, named by its `data-dictation-state`: `load-needed` (the model is not loaded: the glyph sits faint with a warning badge on its corner, and the tooltip names what a press sets up, `Set up dictation: one-time 239 MB download (Cmd+Shift+M)` with the size this device would download, or `Set up dictation: load the speech model (Cmd+Shift+M)` when the files are already on the device), `loading` (the load is shown: the mic eases into a `Loading voice model` label with the download's progress as a bar under it, the composer's box carries `data-composer-state="blocked-loading"`, and the text box is disabled and dimmed under a scrim while the paperclip and Send stay live), `ready` (the model is loaded, and a press dictates at once, with no loading step on the way), and `starting`, `listening` and `stopping`. A press on a `load-needed` mic loads the model and shows the load; it never goes on to listen by itself, and a load that ends while shown leaves the mic `ready` with the text box focused. Two short bell tones say when the microphone is open.

A shown load can be dismissed, and nothing is cancelled by it: a press on the label (a button, which takes the keyboard's focus from the fading mic), a press on the dimmed text box (which puts the caret where the press landed), Escape, the mic's shortcut, or the page going out of view hands the box back for typing while the load runs on. A press right behind the one that asked for the load is taken for that press landing twice and dismisses nothing, and a held shortcut's repeats do nothing. While a dismissed load runs on the mic reads `load-needed`, a press shows the label again at the load's progress, and the mic turns `ready` without a sound when the load ends; a failure after a dismissal is not told, while a failure of a shown load is told in the notice, whose Retry asks again and which comes down by itself once the model is loaded. The model is the page's and is kept in step across the workspace's pages: a model that another chat panel loaded turns this one's mic `ready` too. Until a page has found out the model's state as its composer mounts (five seconds at most) the mic is drawn `ready`, so a loaded model never flashes the badge. The screen wake lock a dictation holds is held while a load is shown as well. The chat frontend declares `@tanstack/query-core`, which the voice library already installs, as a dependency of its own.

A message that may hold dictated words now tells the model so. Once dictation has written words into the composer, the message sent from that draft ends with a short note, in a `<dictation-note>` tag pair after the words, saying that parts of it were transcribed from speech and may be misheard, so the model reads a word that does not fit as the word or identifier it sounds like, without remarking on it, and asks only when two readings would lead to different actions. The note stays with a draft through edits and partial deletes, and goes once the box is emptied or the message is sent; a message that reads as a slash command or bash-mode input is sent without it. The note is for the model alone: the chat's bubbles (sent, sending, queued and held) show only the person's words, the tray's notification bubbles, the owner's mention notification and the automatic chat title leave it out, and a message that comes back to the composer (a failed send, queued messages returned by Stop or by a switch) comes back as its words alone, with the note added again when it is next sent.
