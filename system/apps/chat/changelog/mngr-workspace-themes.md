The chat wears the workspace's theme: it follows the theme its desktop wears (including Desktop settings' live preview), opens in it without a flash of the standard look, and its inner pages follow the outer one.

- The chat declares `[theming] mode = "parts"` with three parts a theme may style: `chat.user-message` (the user's bubbles), `chat.composer`, and `chat.tool-call`. Classic Mac and Windows 2000 style them.
