The Claude model picker's Sonnet and Haiku entries now select Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Haiku 5.5. On the new Claude Code 2.1.293 pin, the `sonnet[1m]` alias resolves to `claude-sonnet-5-5` and the `haiku` alias to `claude-haiku-5-5`, so the entries are labelled "Sonnet 5.5" and "Haiku 5.5" and their `harness_reported_model_id`s move with them. Sonnet 5 (`claude-sonnet-5`) and Haiku 4.5 (`claude-haiku-4-5`) stay in the catalog as hidden options, so a chat created on an earlier pin still shows its model's name. Sonnet 5 is ordered after the offered Sonnet 5.5 key because `claude-sonnet-5` is a prefix of `claude-sonnet-5-5`, and Haiku 4.5 sits ahead of the `claude-haiku-4` family catch-all.

The baked model catalog fixture (now `baked_model_catalog_v2_1_293.json`) and the list of model ids the binary carries were regenerated from the 2.1.293 executable.
