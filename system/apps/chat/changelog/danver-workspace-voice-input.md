The composer dictates. A mic button sits left of Send (and Cmd+Shift+M, or Ctrl+Shift+M off a Mac, works anywhere in the chat page): speech is turned into text on the user's own device, by a model the page downloads from Hugging Face at a pinned commit and runs in a worker every chat page of the workspace shares, so the workspace itself pays no memory for voice (the workspace-voice-input spec in the mngr repository). Dictated words land at the caret, fitted to the sentence around them; unfinished words are rewritten in place until they are final, and only final words are saved to the draft. The box is read-only while listening, and the mic's capsule fills with the input level, in the neutral pressed tint. Enter or Send while dictating finishes the words first, then sends; Escape drops the words that are not yet final. Dictation has no length limit. The speech model's download shows as a ring on the mic with its progress in the tooltip; a problem (a blocked, missing, or busy microphone, a failed download, words that could not be transcribed) shows in a notice over the composer, with Retry where it can help. Hiding the page stops dictation and keeps its words; closing it drops the unfinished ones; a dictation started in another chat of the workspace stops this one.

The chat page's Content-Security-Policy gains `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` in `script-src`, which compiling the speech model's WebAssembly needs and which admits nothing else, and Hugging Face's hosts (`https://huggingface.co` and `https://*.hf.co`) in `connect-src`, for the model's download. The manifest declares `uses_microphone = true`, so the shell hands the microphone to the chat's frames, and the chat root's inner frames allow it too.
