Deleting a chat now takes it off the list the moment you confirm, rather than crossing it out while the delete runs. If the chat was on screen you move to the next one straight away; if the delete is refused, the chat comes back and a dialog over the list says why, with Try again, in place of the browser alert. Deleting the last chat clears the list at once, and the new empty chat opens once the delete has gone through.

Renaming a chat now updates the window's title at once, along with the list, rather than when the next chat list arrives. A chat still waiting for its first message or still starting shows its name in the window's title too, rather than "Chats".

A refused rename now says why in the same dialog, with Rename again to reopen the name field on what you typed. Before, the reason was only a tooltip on a red-ringed field, and nothing at all was shown when another chat's name field was open; that refusal now shows once the other field closes.
