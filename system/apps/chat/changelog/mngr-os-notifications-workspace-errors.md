When a chat can't reply because its AI account is signed out (an expired sign-in or a revoked key), the chat now tells the Imbue Studio app. You get a notification (the bell, a toast, and a system banner when you're looking elsewhere), and clicking it opens the chat, where the reply has the sign-in button. A chat that keeps failing sends one notification, not one per message, until a reply goes through again. If you're already looking at the chat, nothing pops up.
