A reply the agent is still writing now shows where its words will land. While a step is open, the text streams in as that step's caption (or at the end of its work, when the step is expanded), the way it reads once it lands, rather than at the bottom of the chat outside the step. With no step open it shows below the chat as before.

A Claude agent's streamed reply no longer repeats text the chat already shows. Once a run of tool calls finished, Claude's terminal summarizes them as "Ran 4 shell commands" under the text before them, and that summary was read as part of a new reply, so the earlier text showed a second time with the summary attached. While a background helper agent ran, the "main" row of Claude's agent list could also show as a reply. Both come from the mngr Claude plugin's terminal reader, fixed in the mngr commit this template now pins.

A step the agent left open at the end of one turn no longer swallows its answer to your next message, or the answer of the agent you switch the chat to. What the agent says goes to the normal reply until it works on that step again, and a turn that never returns to the step does not show it again, so a quick follow-up question is answered as a reply rather than as a caption under an unrelated, still-spinning step.
