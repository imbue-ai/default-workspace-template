The chat app has a phone layout, taken whenever the chat window is 700px wide or less (on a phone, or a narrow window on a desktop):

- A 44px header shows a list button, the title of the chat on screen, and a kebab offering Rename, Stop chat or Restart chat, and Delete chat for that chat. A rename from the header is typed in place of the title.

- The chat list is a drawer over the chat instead of a strip of monograms. Its rows are the list's rows as on a desktop (status dot, helpers nested under their lead, stopped chats faded with the pause mark, unread chats green with a check), and each carries a kebab with the same verbs the right-click menu offers. "New chat" is a plus in the drawer's header. A tap on the scrim, Escape, or dragging the drawer to the left closes it; with no chat selected it is open over an empty chat that says to pick or start one.

- The model menu opens from a settings button at the left of the composer instead of the chip under it. It holds the same rows (Provider with its accounts, Model, Effort, Fast Mode, Stop agent) plus the Source view switch; its submenus slide in over the card instead of opening beside it, and the effort is a row of segments instead of a slider. While the terminal face is showing, the Source view switch sits under the composer as before, to turn back.

- The chat list's collapse toggle and its collapsed strip of monograms are gone on every layout.
