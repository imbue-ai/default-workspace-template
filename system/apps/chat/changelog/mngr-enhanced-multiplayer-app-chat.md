The chat about an app (the mngr repo's `specs/multiplayer-refinements/spec.md`, section 9.2):

- The manifest declares `app-chat` ("Chat about this app", `POST /api/chats/intake`, params `app` and `app_display_name`, preset `target = "app_chat"`, `app_params = {name = "app", display_name = "app_display_name"}`), which a window's title bar balloon runs; never a launcher row.

- The new `app_chat` intake target answers the chat titled exactly "<app_display_name> Chat", of either kind or still being made, else creates one with that title on the default account (never auto-named), else, with nothing signed in, mints one awaiting its first send under the title (`mint_awaiting_chat` takes a name). The intake is always held, even with nothing to send, with a new `hint` ("How do you want to change <app>?") on the pending intake's view and its apply answer; the chat root puts the hint on the chat's composer as its placeholder until the next send and focuses it (`setComposerHint`, the page's embed API's `hintComposer`). An applied intake with nothing to draft or launch now answers `null` for `composer_text` and `first_message` rather than an empty string. The chat app's own windows open "Chat Chat".
