The chat pages wear the Windows 95 look: the chat list and the transcript sit in sunken white wells on a silver ground, the selected chat is navy, bubbles are square, the composer is a sunken field with a bevelled Send button, and the transcript's scrollbar is a grey track with a raised thumb. The theme is one stylesheet, `frontend/src/win95.css`, imported after the shared tokens; the markup is unchanged. The chat's icon is a white pixel-art speech bubble that takes the colour of the text beside it.
