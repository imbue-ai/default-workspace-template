Two Antigravity chats that both show queued messages no longer hang the chat app when their queues change at the same moment, such as a send to one while the other's held messages are being typed. A chat's queue announced each change while holding its lock, and that announcement asked every other Antigravity chat, under that chat's own lock, whether its shoulder tap was available. Two such announcements could each wait on the other's lock forever, and every later chat-list request, send, stop or tap on those chats hung until the chat app restarted. A chat's queue now answers whether it is sending without taking its lock.
