The composer takes the shape of the chat-input prototype. The box is tighter, with a 2px accent border on focus and corners that follow the buttons inside it. Its text takes the message's own size and line height. Its row reads paperclip | text | send, with square 28px buttons and a finer paperclip; once the text wraps it takes the full width, with the paperclip at the bottom left and send at the bottom right. Send stays put with nothing to send, instead of disappearing: just its arrow, faded, until there is something to send and it fills in green.

Attachments sit between the text and the buttons, on one row of 40px chips that scrolls sideways when they don't fit, with < / > arrows over soft edge fades, and scrolls to a chip as it is added. An image is its thumbnail alone; a file shows its icon, name and size, at most 160px wide. The remove button is a badge centred on the chip's corner, shown on hover. While a file uploads, a spinner sits over an image's own thumbnail, or where a file's icon goes, with its size still shown.

The transcript, the composer and the bar under it share one 672px column, 24px from the sides of the window, so the composer no longer runs wider than the messages above it.
