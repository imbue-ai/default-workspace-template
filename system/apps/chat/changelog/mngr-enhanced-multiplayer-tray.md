The chat app feeds the desktop's tray and makes the Lounge (the mngr repo's `specs/multiplayer-refinements/spec.md`, sections 6 and 7.5):

- Every message a person or a visiting agent sends, and every turn the workspace agent closes with text (its post into a room included), is posted to the shell as a speech bubble (`POST /api/bubbles`, with the people a room message mentions), and every chat-list change is posted as what each chat is doing (`POST /api/chats-activity`), both best-effort and off the request thread. A secondary chat posts neither.

- The room titled "Lounge" is made the first time the roster holds anyone besides the owner (a person, an invited email, or a visiting agent), from the roster the shell answers (read on every page and send, and every minute), and again when a click on a person needs it after it was deleted. The new `lounge` intake target resolves to it, making it when there is none, and the manifest's `lounge` launch path (`mention_param = "message"`) drafts `@<handle> ` into it when a person's tray entry is clicked.
