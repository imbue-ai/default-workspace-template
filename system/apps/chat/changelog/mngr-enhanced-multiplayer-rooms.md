Chat rooms and mentions (the mngr repo's `specs/multiplayer-refinements/spec.md`, section 7):

- A room is a chat among the people in the workspace with no AI agent. A chat waiting for its first send becomes one from its provider chooser ("Chat room (no provider)"), and any chat but the main chat becomes a room, or a room becomes an agent chat, from the composer's provider menu: the switch keeps the conversation so far as the chat's earlier segments, and a chat leaving a room hands its new agent the room's transcript. Rooms sit in the chat list with the other chats, wearing a people glyph, and take every chat verb (rename, delete, stop, start). A message sent into a stopped room is appended to its transcript without reviving its reader, so the room keeps reading as stopped until `mngr message <room>`, the row's Restart, or boot starts the reader again.

- A room is an mngr agent of the template's new `room` type: its process reads what `mngr message <room>` types into its pane into the room's transcript, which `mngr transcript <room>` renders and the chat app tails. The chat app appends people's messages to the same transcript, so every message in a room is drawn as its author's turn under their name and picture, the owner's unmarked.

- Typing `@` in a room's composer opens a list of handles: the people here, `@agent` for the workspace's agent, and every chat by its title (`@Main-chat`). Mentioning `@agent` or a chat from a room sends that chat the message, marked as said in the room, and the room's row shows a working dot until the agent answers into the room (a chat that refuses the message, as one whose account was signed out does, is not waited on). Mentioning a person marks the room's row with an `@` for them until they look at it, and tells the workspace's owner through the Imbue Studio app's feed as the room itself, so the notification opens the room. In an agent chat a handle is a word the agent reads, and the composer offers no popover.

- The chat snapshot carries `kind` (`agent` or `room`) and `awaiting_chat_ids`; `POST /api/chats/create` and `POST /api/chats/<id>/handoff` take `kind` (a room is refused beside an account or a model, before anything is written); `GET /api/mentions` and `GET /api/mentions/pending` are new, with the `mentions_updated` push on `/api/ws`. There are no room-only routes, no room store, and no second chat id shape.
