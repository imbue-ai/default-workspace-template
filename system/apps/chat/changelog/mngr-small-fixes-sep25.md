The provider chooser describes Opencode Go and OpenRouter in our own words instead of echoing the providers' marketing, and no longer quotes their prices: Opencode Go is "A subscription to a selection of open models." and OpenRouter is "Models from many providers on one key." The Opencode Go key screen now reads "One key for every model on your Opencode Go subscription."

A message you queue while a Claude agent is working no longer disappears when the agent's reply comes in. The agent looks idle the moment its reply lands, but Claude only picks up the queued message after its end-of-turn hooks run; in that gap the chat used to drop the message and show it again once its turn started. It now stays below the reply, shown as being sent, until its turn arrives. A message still queued 10 seconds after the agent went idle is treated as stranded and removed, as before.
