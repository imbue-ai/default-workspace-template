The "switch to another provider" link under a failed chat turn now moves that chat to the account you pick, the same way the provider menu does, instead of opening a new chat. Picking an account on another harness opens the switch dialog, so the conversation is handed off. Picking another account on the same harness and provider arms a rebind, which keeps the agent and its conversation. Either way your next message carries out the switch. Accounts that are already signed in can now be picked from this chooser directly, where before the only options were signing in to a new provider or signing in again. The failed chat's own account is listed as "Not working" and cannot be picked; its "Sign in again" and remove buttons still work. The link only appears once the page has loaded the chat. "+ Add a provider" in the composer's provider menu also lets you pick a signed-in account now, with the chat's current account marked "Current" and not pickable. A new chat that is waiting for an account can likewise start on one that is already signed in.
