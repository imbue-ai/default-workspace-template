The browser's manifest declares `stop_when_no_windows = false`: Chromium still stops on the close hint, but the coordinator program keeps running, since agents drive browsers with no window at all (the agentic-browser-fleet skill, through the CDP proxy) and stopping the coordinator would close every Chromium it runs. Quit from a browser window's menu still closes its windows and stops the program, and the shell holds its port while it is stopped (`docs/system/specs/stop-when-no-windows.md`).
