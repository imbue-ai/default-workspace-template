The File Viewer is now Files, rebuilt to feel like Finder or the Windows Files app instead of a directory listing.

- A resizable sidebar with Shortcuts (Workspace, Uploads) and Connected: every file and folder shared from your computers. Each one's icon carries a small badge saying whether it is linked (opened live from your computer) or synced (a copy kept here), and the same badge marks it wherever a folder lists it. After the name, a "Read-only" badge, an orange alert when a synced copy hasn't caught up, or a crossed-out laptop when your computer isn't answering; hovering says how it syncs and how it stands. With nothing shared yet, it says to share from Imbue Studio on your computer. Dropping something from your computer onto a shared folder's row uploads into it. A toolbar with the path, a view switcher, a + button, and view options.

- Files has a server of its own in place of dufs: it serves the workspace's files, downloads a folder as a zip as it is written, saves an upload whole or not at all (a cut-short upload leaves the old file), keeps an edited file's permissions and links, reads the shares through the latchkey gateway, opens unsynced ones live from your computer, and keeps file names with unusual characters (like the narrow space in macOS screenshot names) from breaking their preview. dufs is no longer installed.

- Three views: icons (images as thumbnails), a list with Name, Date Modified, Size, and Kind columns that sort, and columns that open to the right as folders are chosen and end in a preview of the chosen file. The view, the sort order, and whether system files show are remembered.

- Click to select (Cmd/Ctrl and Shift to select several), double-click or Enter to open, F2 to rename in place, Delete to delete after a confirmation, and keyboard arrows, Cmd/Ctrl+A, Cmd/Alt+Up, and type-to-find.

- Right-click an item for Open, Open in New Window, Edit, Download (folders as a zip), Rename, Copy Path, and Delete; right-click empty space, or use +, for New Folder, New File, Upload Files, and Upload Folder.

- Drag files or folders in from your computer to upload them into the folder under the pointer, with progress shown as they go (the panel tidies itself away once they are done); a name already there asks whether to replace it or keep both. Drag items onto a folder to move them.

- A file opens in a window of its own, to be closed when you are done: it previews pictures, video, audio, and PDFs, shows text, and edits it (the Edit icon beside Download) with Save or Cmd/Ctrl+S; it also offers Rename, Copy Path, and Delete.

- On a phone-sized window the sidebar floats over the folder from a toolbar button, the column view steps aside, a tap opens, and each item has a "..." menu.

The column preview of a text file and a text file's own page use the shared type scale (helper and body sizes) rather than hand-set pixel sizes, and the list's sortable column headers drop the medium weight, as buttons do.

A note Files leaves when something fails (a move, a save, a copy) rises at the foot of the window like the desktop's, several stack rather than replace one another, and each can be closed. The pointer hides while you type, as everywhere else in the workspace.
