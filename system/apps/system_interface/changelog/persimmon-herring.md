The shell's menus are the workspace's shared Menu (`workspace_ui/components/menu`), described as rows rather than built by hand.

The project rail's five menus -- the project switcher under the header, the header's context menu, the row and shortcut kebab menus, and the All apps popover -- each become a `createMenu` instance with a list of rows. The rail loses its own scrim, floating card, row recipe and placement (`placeMenu` moves to the library as `menu-position.ts`, where the tab menu was already importing it from), and its outside-press and Escape listeners: the shared menu closes on a press on its own sheet or on Escape, and on nothing else. The rail's expansion follows a menu closing unless the pointer is still on the rail, since a menu floats beside the rail and the pointer is usually off it by the time the menu closes. "New project" keeps the menu up, so a create that fails can say so on the line under the rows.

The tab kebab menu is the same Menu rendered into a root of its own on <body> -- the tab strip is dockview's DOM rather than mithril's -- and loses its hand-built rows and its scroll and resize listeners: with a sheet under it nothing behind it can scroll, and a menu opened by a click closes on a click or Escape. The launcher's per-section filter is the same Menu again, its app rows as check rows that toggle without closing and its reset as a quiet action row, in place of the launcher's own document listeners.

Every menu keeps its test hooks (`.project-rail-menu`, `role="menuitem"`), which now live on <body> where the menus portal to; the shell's tests read them off the document rather than the rail's root.
