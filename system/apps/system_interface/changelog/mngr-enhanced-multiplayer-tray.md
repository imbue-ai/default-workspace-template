The taskbar's tray becomes where the people and the agent live (the mngr repo's `specs/multiplayer-refinements/spec.md`, section 6):

- The tray is the share button (a dashed circle with a plus, drawn only under an Imbue Studio chrome, which it asks to open the share panel on the whole workspace), one picture per person in the roster (faded while they are away, a dashed envelope for an invited email, a monogram tile for a visiting agent, stacked from four on and spread while the pointer is over them), your own picture, and the agent's avatar. An entry hops when it arrives and when a bubble of its appears. The desktop switcher moved to the left of the window entries, where the desktops phase puts the overview button.

- Hovering a person opens their card: the name, whether they are active, idle, offline, or invited, what they are looking at (the window at the top of the stack on their newest client), and their desktops, with "New desktop" on your own. Hovering the agent opens its card: the workspace name with a pencil to the avatar chooser, whether it is working, and the chats it is working on and the ones waiting on you, which open on a click. Clicking a person drafts a mention of them into the Lounge through the first launch path of the machine that declares a `mention_param`; clicking the agent opens the main chat.

- Speech bubbles: the chat app posts every message somebody sends and every turn the agent closes with text to `POST /api/bubbles`, and the shell broadcasts each as `bubble` to every window but the speaker's own clients. A bubble hangs over the speaker's entry for five seconds after its last change; a person's new message replaces theirs, the agent's new message within its bubble's lifetime is appended ("and N more" past three lines), bubbles of different speakers stack, and a mention of the viewer wears an accent border and stays until dismissed or its chat is opened. A click opens the chat it was said in.

- `POST /api/chats-activity` takes what every chat is doing from the chat app, kept and broadcast as `chats_activity` (sent on connect once reported); `roster_updated` is read by the page now, so the tray draws the roster rather than the connected users alone.
