The shell now knows who is here. Every open shell window heartbeats `POST /api/presence/heartbeat` (every 30 seconds while visible, with a leave beacon on unload), and the shell records each signed-in user's identity -- as the request's `X-Imbue-Identity` header carries it -- under `data/.state/presence/users/<user_id>.json`, appends `user_joined` / `user_left` events to `data/.state/presence/events.jsonl`, serves the connected set at `GET /api/presence`, pushes it as `presence_updated` over the WebSocket, and draws one avatar per connected user in the corner of the window. Over a share the strip links to the gateway's identity refresh. The owner of an unshared workspace carries no identity and is not recorded.
