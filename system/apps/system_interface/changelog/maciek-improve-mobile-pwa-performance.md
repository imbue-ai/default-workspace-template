On a phone, the workspace no longer flashes its home screen before opening on the chat: until the page knows where it lands it shows a plain background, and it lands as soon as it knows its desktop, without waiting for that desktop's layout. Coming back to the workspace after the phone slept reconnects at once instead of waiting out a reconnect delay that can grow to 30 seconds while the phone is asleep. The page's built files are now cached by the browser for good, since their names change with their contents, so reopening the workspace no longer asks for them again.
