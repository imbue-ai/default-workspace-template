The desktop wears the Imbue brand, colored from the workspace color.

- The shell reads the workspace color (the `color` label Imbue Studio keeps on the workspace's primary agent) when it starts and whenever Imbue Studio says it changed (`minds:workspace-color`), and every window recolors without a reload.
- The default wallpaper is the "studio" wordmark rising off the bottom of a field in the workspace color's wallpaper tint. It is listed first among the wallpapers.
- A window's title bar is a step back from the page when it has focus and a shade darker behind it; desktop shortcuts sit flat, with their names in body text.
- Every view uses the new color roles.
- Type is the brand's: Atkinson Hyperlegible for text, Besley for headings.
- The launcher field is the brand's 32px field with 12px corners; the tray cards are the shared floating card.
