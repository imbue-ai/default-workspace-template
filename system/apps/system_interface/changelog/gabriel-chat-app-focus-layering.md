Bringing a window to the front by clicking its content, or by any other route that is not a press on its title bar or edges (an agent focusing it, a link opening it, a menu's Focus last), now takes the keyboard off the window it covered. Before, that window kept the keyboard: typing went into a window you could no longer see, and switching to another application and back put it back on top of the one you had chosen (the chat window resurfacing over a maximized app).
