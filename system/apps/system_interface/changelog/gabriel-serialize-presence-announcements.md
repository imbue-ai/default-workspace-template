The people shown as connected no longer get stuck on an out-of-date list. When a person's profile finished loading at the moment someone else arrived or left, the announcement carrying the profile could reach the windows after the one carrying that change, leaving them with the older list until the next change. The shell now reads and sends each update of who is connected one at a time, so the newest list always arrives last. And someone a profile update showed as gone, because their window had briefly stopped checking in, now reappears as soon as it checks in again, rather than staying gone until someone else arrived or left.
