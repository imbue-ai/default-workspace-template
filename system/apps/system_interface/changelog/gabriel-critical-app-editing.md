`app.toml` gains the shell's `[preview]` table: a preview boots `system-interface --preview --state-dir {copy:state}` over a seeded copy of `data/.state/system_interface`, reads a copied registry through `MINDS_APPS_FILE = {registry}`, and probes `/api/health`.

`system-interface --preview` boots the shell as a read-only preview of a proposed change: over the state directory it is given (a seeded copy) and the registry `MINDS_APPS_FILE` names, it refuses every relay verb, the app-level stop and start, and a create through the op route with a 403 naming the preview, says `is_preview` in its inventory document, marks its page with the `system-interface-preview` meta tag, and raises no staleness banner. The frontend reads the tag and hides the verbs the backend refuses (rename, delete, stop, and start on the tab and rail menus, the New Tab tiles, the picker's rows, and a stopped app's Start); tab, filing, and pinning verbs stay, since they edit the preview's own copy.

The README's "Updating the running UI" describes the careful flow the shell's `critical = true` routes every edit through, with `preview_app.py` in place of the removed reveal script and `--keep-rollback-point` on the apply.
