The speech bubbles over the taskbar's tray now show only what is meant for you: a chat's notification to the user (over the agent's entry, on every client not looking at that chat, timing out after five seconds like any bubble) and a message that mentions you, from a person, a visiting agent, or the agent's post into a room (marked, and kept until dismissed or its chat is opened). Any other message still makes its sender's entry hop but raises no bubble. The `bubble` push carries a new `kind` (`message` or `notification`).
