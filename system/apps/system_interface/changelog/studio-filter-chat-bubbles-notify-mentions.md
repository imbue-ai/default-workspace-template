The speech bubbles over the taskbar's tray now show only what is meant for you: a chat's notification to the user (over the agent's entry, on every client not looking at that chat, timing out after five seconds like any bubble) and a message that mentions you (marked, and kept until dismissed or its chat is opened). Other messages, the agent's own replies included, no longer raise a bubble; they still make the speaker's entry hop. The `bubble` push carries a new `kind` (`message` or `notification`).
