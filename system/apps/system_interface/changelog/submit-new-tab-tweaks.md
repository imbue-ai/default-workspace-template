Polish on the New Tab page redesign that shipped in `mngr-new-tab-page.md`, all of it in how the page answers the pointer and how it holds up as its pane narrows.

- Both of the page's offers now float off the page under the pointer on one shared 300ms ease-out, and each grows exactly one thing inside it -- its picture. On a template card that is the drawing, as before; on a "Start something" tile it is the glyph, and the tile itself holds still, because a tile is mostly text and a paragraph on the move under the pointer is harder to read than an icon on the move. The glyph grows from its left edge so it stays lined up with the text beneath it. The tile's hover tint is gone: it sat behind the new shadow and muted it, so a tile read as highlighted where a card read as raised. A tile that is standing down still stays flat, so the page never offers to open what it cannot.

- A template rail's paging arrows have moved out of the scrolling area into a sliver at either end, out in the page's margin, so the cards run to the rail's edge with nothing over them and still line up under their heading. The whole sliver pages the rail, the full height of the row, so the arrow costs no aim; what lights up under the pointer is only a 24px circle, sitting level with the middle of the card drawings rather than the middle of the row. Each sliver stays in the layout whether or not it holds an arrow, so reaching either end of a rail does not resize the cards under the pointer.

- Each rail end that can still be scrolled towards fades out, as the rest of saying "there is more this way". The fade is an overlay in the page's own colour laid over the scroller's edge, not a mask, so showing and hiding it is opacity, and `pointer-events: none` keeps the rail scrolling and the cards clickable underneath. Its stops are eased rather than plain -- `(1-x)^2.2` sampled at twelfths over 30px -- because the overlay is linear in its alpha while apparent lightness is not: a near-black card crosses about 84 points of L* over the fade, where a two-stop ramp bands visibly. Only an end with rail past it is faded, so the two cards that come to rest against an edge are never dimmed and keep their room to lift.

- The rail's cards, its edge fade and its arrows are ordered with z-indexes now rather than paint order, and the row is an `isolate` so none of it reaches the rest of the page. A hovered card was coming out cut off by a hard edge: the lift transforms the card, which puts it in the positioned layer, where it beat the overlay on DOM order -- and paint order cannot express "above one sibling, below another", which is what the fade needs (over the cards, under the arrow circles that hang back into it).

- The page's tiles and cards step down as the pane narrows, where before everything was sized for one width and splitting a pane to a third of the window turned each row into slivers. "Open new" goes four to a row, then three below a 760px pane, two below 620px and one below 260px; "Start something" three, then two below 760px and one below 480px; a rail's cards three and a half, then two and a half below 620px and one and a half below 440px, with the gap tightening from 24px to 16px alongside. These are CONTAINER queries against the pane, not media queries against the window, because this page is a dock panel: it can be split to a sliver while the window stays wide, and a media query would keep it four-up the whole way down.
