An `open` op with nothing to dock beside now tabs into the group the client already has active, instead of splitting a fresh column open beside it. "Beside" needs an anchor, and the anchor is the requester's own instance; when the requester has no docked panel -- the chat app's reactor surfacing a chat the Minds app started, or any agent surfacing its *own* chat, which is by definition not docked yet -- the op fell back to the active group and docked to its right. That group is usually the rightmost one, so there was nothing to its right to tab into and the op split a new column; and because the new tab became active in its own group, the next such open split again beside it. Four app-launched chats meant four columns.

An `open` whose requester *is* docked is unchanged: it still lands to that panel's right, tabbing into a group already there, which is what an agent asking for a tab next to its own chat wants. `--new-group` still forces a column of its own either way.
