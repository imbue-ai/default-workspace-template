The presence strip from the share identity work is now a tray widget (`data-tray-widget="presence"`) at the taskbar's right end, beside the Desktops widget, rather than a fixed overlay in the corner of the window: the desktop's taskbar owns the bottom edge, so the connected users' avatars and the "Refresh my profile" link sit in the tray. The `presence_updated` push reaches the desktop store through the shell socket like every other message.
