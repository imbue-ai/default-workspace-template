The shell's pages (the desktop and the not-built placeholder) now carry a `Content-Security-Policy`: scripts only from the shell's own origin plus the placeholder's inline script by hash, fetches and sockets only to the shell, frames of any http(s) page (the apps it shows), images from its own origin (its wallpapers and icons), no plugins. Who may frame the shell stays the proxy's `frame-ancestors` policy. The page a parked port answers for a stopped app carries one too, admitting its inline stylesheet and nothing else.
