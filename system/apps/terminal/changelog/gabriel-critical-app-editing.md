`app.toml` gains the terminal's `[preview]` table: a preview boots `terminal-app --no-register` with the wrapper pages on a free port, over a copy of `data/.apps/terminal` and a scratch state directory, reading the registry copy `MINDS_APPS_FILE = {registry}` names, and probes `/api/health`. The pages frame the pty the registry names, so a terminal preview is booted `--with terminal-pty`; the pty's own manifest (`system/apps/terminal_pty/app.toml`) gains a `[preview]` table that boots `terminal-pty --no-register` on a free port with its dispatch scripts under a scratch state directory, probed at ttyd's own page.

`terminal-app --no-register` and `terminal-pty --no-register` serve without re-pointing the live rows or appending the `server_registered` discovery event (a preview inherits `MNGR_AGENT_STATE_DIR` from the shell that booted it, and its throwaway URL is not the workspace's terminal). An unregistered terminal also runs no window sweep: the sweep is grounded in the windows the shell shows of the registered app, and a preview's windows are another app's, so it would collect exactly the terminals only the preview shows.
