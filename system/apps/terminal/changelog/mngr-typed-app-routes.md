The terminal's routes are typed: `POST /new` reads a `NewTerminalRequest` (an optional `workdir`) and answers a `NewTerminalResponse` (the new terminal's page path), `GET /api/sessions/{name}` answers a `SessionPage`, `GET /api/health` a `HealthResponse`, and `POST /api/window-closed` reads the shell's `WindowClosedHint` and answers 204; the page and the app contract module are declared schemaless. The API document at `/api/schema` publishes every model, every parse and answer is checked against the route table, and a body of another shape than a route's model (a window-closed post that is not the shell's hint included) is refused as a 400 `{"detail": ...}` rather than tolerated.
