The terminal's pages install the request guard ahead of their routes (`app_schema.request_guard`): a request the workspace's own browser marks as a page of another origin's is refused with 403, and a request with no `X-Imbue-Identity` header reads as the owner's only from loopback under a loopback `Host`, so a page shown on the Screen's browser can no longer open a terminal as the owner (imbue-ai/mngr-internal#2816). The shell's launch of a terminal and its window-closed posts come over loopback and are unaffected.
