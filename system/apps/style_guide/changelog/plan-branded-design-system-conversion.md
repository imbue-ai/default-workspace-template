Shows the Imbue brand's color roles, and passes the workspace color on to its page-kit sample with the theme. Its type section lists the brand's whole ramp.

- Colors shows each ramp as a strip of its twelve steps and lists the roles without their computed shades, and a picker previews any workspace color (Imbue Studio's, or any hex) on the page alone, through `/api/workspace-color/preview`.
- Fields, notices, toasts and buttons show the new controls: Checkbox, Switch, the error edge, notice and toast tones, and the large and accent buttons.
