Chats are now protected while they launch. A chat used to be tagged into the middle of the chat band at launch and only pulled down toward the protected floor once the user engaged with it, but none of the signals that earn that protection -- an open tab, a visible tab, a recent message -- can exist in a chat's first seconds. That made a brand-new chat the most expendable chat in the workspace during the one window where a shed is unrecoverable: it has no transcript to revive into, and its opening message is in flight and unrepeatable.

A chat now launches at `CHAT_AGENT_LAUNCH` (the protected floor) and `chat_agent_oom_score_adj` holds it there while its process is younger than `CHAT_LAUNCH_GRACE_SECONDS`, so the chat app's prioritizer agrees with the launch wrapper instead of undoing it on the first pass. Past the grace the ordinary engagement and staleness scoring applies unchanged, and a chat whose process start time cannot be read gets no grace at all.

Two limits worth knowing. The floor still sits above every service band, so this reorders a launching chat against workers, agent subprocesses and other chats but never puts it below the workspace's own services -- on a freshly booted workspace with no other agents, a launching chat remains the highest-banded process in the container. And a chat that stops being re-tagged now rests at the floor rather than mid-band, which is the one place this policy fails *protected* rather than expendable: it sits above a worker doing unrecoverable work until the next sweep re-scores it.
