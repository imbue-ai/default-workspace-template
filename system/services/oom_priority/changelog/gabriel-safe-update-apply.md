New near-exempt band `UPDATE_APPLY` (15) for the update-apply orchestrator: above every agent, chat, and ordinary service, below the owner-exec (5) and terminal (10) authority bands that would repair a failed apply. Losing one of the apply's builds is an ordinary failure its rollback absorbs; losing the orchestrator mid-motion strands the workspace half-applied with the only copies of the pre-apply state orphaned.
