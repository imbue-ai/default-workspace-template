earlyoom's scoring model (`predict_ranking`, the `/proc/<pid>/status` and `smaps` readers, the `--avoid` reader) moved from `bin/oom_drill.py` into the stdlib-only `oom_priority.badness` module, with a reader for the running earlyoom's argv, so the drill and other callers predict the same victim from one implementation. The drill's behaviour is unchanged; it is now sent into a workspace together with that module as a small tarball (`OOM_DRILL.md`), and a non-UTF-8 `smaps` or argv no longer drops a process from its snapshot.
