The resource sampler now records who holds the memory, by the same chats and programs it credits CPU to. Every ten minutes it appends the five largest holders, by anonymous memory, to `events/memory_holders/events.jsonl`. A memory stretch (memory at 85% or more for a minute, until it stays under 80% for a minute) appends a `memory_stretch_opened` event with its holders at once and a `memory_stretch` event with the holders at its peak when it closes (`events/memory_stretches/events.jsonl`).
