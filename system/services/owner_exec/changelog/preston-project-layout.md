Registers itself with `forward_port.py --internal` instead of a plain registration. owner-exec has a port to forward (the share gateway and the desktop's forward route through it) but no page of its own -- registering plainly put it in the workspace's app list, where it showed up as an openable app in the New Tab launcher and the rail's All apps popover despite having nothing to open. `--internal` (new in `system/scripts/forward_port.py`) keeps the port routable while telling the frontend not to offer it.
