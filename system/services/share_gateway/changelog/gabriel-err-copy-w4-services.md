A share-link visitor is no longer told "Not shared with you" for things that are not a sharing problem. An address this workspace does not serve (an old or mistyped link, an app that was removed) now says "Nothing is shared at this address right now. Check the link with the person who sent it." A real refusal says "This page hasn't been shared with your account (name@example.com). Ask the person who shared it to give you access, then reload this page.", naming the account the visitor is signed in as, with a "Reload" button and a "Sign in with a different account" button. That button posts to a new `/_auth/switch-account` route, which ends the visitor's session on this workspace and sends them back through Imbue's sign-in with `prompt=select_account`, so they can pick a different account even where sign-in would otherwise put the same one straight back; the route takes only a POST from one of this workspace's own pages, so a link from another site cannot sign anyone out. A request the gateway refuses because it came from another site now says "This page can't be opened from another site" / "For your security, this workspace doesn't accept requests sent from other websites. Open the page directly instead." with an "Open this page" button, instead of "Not shared with you ... your account has not been granted access".

A shared app that has stopped answering, or that caddy has not routed yet, no longer shows "Starting up..." forever. Each case has its own page, which shows a spinner and reloads every 3 seconds for up to a minute, then stops with a "Try again" button: "This page isn't responding right now. Let the person who shared it know, then try again." for an app that stopped answering (any error status caddy raises, not only a refused connection), and "This page still isn't available. Let the person who shared it know." for one not routed yet. For an app the workspace never restarts on its own (a critical app, an agent's server, an address off the workspace's own machine) the page says "This app isn't responding. The workspace owner may need to restart it." The owner and anyone with access to the whole workspace read "Ask an agent in the workspace to restart it, then try again." instead ("...to look into it..." for an app not routed yet), and get an "Open the workspace" button when the page fills the browser tab. When the workspace's own interface is what is down, the page says "This workspace isn't responding." and tells the owner to open it in the Imbue Studio app, which will try to restart it, and everyone else that the workspace owner may need to restart it. A form post or other non-GET request to such an app gets the page too, instead of a "405 Method Not Allowed".

An app list (`apps.toml`) that cannot be read no longer makes every address read as unshared: the gateway, caddy and the relays keep the last list they read, and before any good read every address says "This page isn't available right now. Try again in a moment." Caddy now strips a client-sent `X-Imbue-Identity` on every request, not just the ones it hands to an app.
