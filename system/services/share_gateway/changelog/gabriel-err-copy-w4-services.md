A share-link visitor is no longer told "Not shared with you" for things that are not a sharing problem. An address this workspace does not serve (an old or mistyped link, an app that was removed) now says "Nothing is shared at this address right now. Check the link with the person who sent it." A real refusal says "This page hasn't been shared with your account. Ask the person who shared it to give you access, then reload this page." with a "Reload" button and a "Sign in with a different account" button. That button posts to a new `/_auth/switch-account` route, which ends the visitor's session on this workspace and sends them back through Imbue's sign-in, where they can pick a different account; the route takes only a POST from one of this workspace's own pages, so a link from another site cannot sign anyone out. A request the gateway refuses because it came from another site now says "This page can't be opened from another site" / "For your security, this workspace doesn't accept requests sent from other websites. Open the page directly instead." with an "Open this page" button, instead of "Not shared with you ... your account has not been granted access".

A shared app that has stopped answering no longer shows "Starting up..." forever. Caddy now sends it to its own not-responding page, which reloads every 3 seconds for up to a minute and then stops on "This page isn't responding right now. Let the person who shared it know, then try again." with a "Try again" button. For an app the workspace never restarts on its own (a critical app, an agent's server, an address off the workspace's own machine), the page says "This app isn't responding. The workspace owner may need to restart it." instead.
