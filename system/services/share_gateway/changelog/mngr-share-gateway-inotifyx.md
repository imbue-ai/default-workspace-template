Sharing now starts as soon as the minds app writes the share materials, instead of up to ten seconds later. The share gateway watched `data/.secrets/share.env` through `inotifyx`, which was never a declared dependency and is not installed in the workspace, so the watch silently fell back to a blind ten-second sleep between polls and every share enable (and unshare) waited out the remainder of a tick before anything happened. The gateway now watches through `inotify_simple`, the library the app_watcher service already uses and the template already ships.\n
