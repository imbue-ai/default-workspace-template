The gateway now enforces app shareability. A per-service grant (`[services.<name>]`) admits a visitor only when the app's registry row is shareable: not `internal`, and not `shareable = false` (the chat, terminal, browser, and Getting Started). On any other app it admits nobody, so those apps are reached only through a workspace-level grant. A visitor whose only grants are on such apps is refused at login rather than signed in to a workspace where every page is forbidden.
