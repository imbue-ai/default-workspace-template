A per-service grant (`[services.<name>]`) now admits a visitor only to an app whose registry row is not `internal` (the shell itself, the terminal's pty, the owner and VM exec services); a per-service grant on an internal app admits nobody, so those are reached only through a workspace-level grant. A visitor whose only grants are on internal apps is refused at login rather than signed in to a workspace where every page is forbidden. Whether an app declares `system` makes no difference to the gateway.
