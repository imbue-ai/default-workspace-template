The rendered Caddyfile serves the gateway's auto-retrying loading page for a registered service whose backend caddy cannot reach (`handle_errors 502`) instead of a bare 502: the moment between an app stopping and the shell parking its port, or between the shell releasing the port and the app binding it (`docs/system/specs/stop-when-no-windows.md`, section 5.7). The share-gateway program runs as its own process (`exec .venv/bin/share-gateway`) rather than under a resident `uv run`.
