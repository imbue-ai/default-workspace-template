The build-app scaffold's starter `runner.py` now serves every HTML page under a `Content-Security-Policy` (scripts only from the app's own origin plus the starter's inline shell-contract script by hash, same-origin fetches, sockets and images, inline styles, frames of any http(s) page, no plugins), with `inline_script_source` for admitting another inline script, a `report-uri` pointing at a new `/_csp-report` route that logs every resource the browser blocked to stderr (so a CDN script, font or API a page pulls in without its origin in the policy shows up in the supervisor log, where `check-app-errors` finds it, instead of failing silently; a blocked inline script is logged with its line and first characters, which the policy's `'report-sample'` asks the browser for, and the hash remedy; a block caused by a browser extension is not logged), and the skill says to keep the policy, that anything from another origin is blocked until the matching directive admits it, and where the log line is.
