update-self now updates a workspace created before the template's history was rewritten to drop the vendored mngr directories. Such a workspace shares no commit with a newer release, so the update's merge had no base. The lead's new `bridge-history` step (end of Step 3a, right after a forced tag fetch) finds the workspace's fork point and its rewritten twin in the target (same author, committer, dates and subject, same tree once the vendored paths are removed) and adds a `git replace` graft naming the fork point as the twin's extra parent. The ordinary worker merge and fast-forward apply then run unchanged, and the workspace's own history is never rewritten. Teardown drops the graft whatever the outcome (`bridge-history --drop`); a retry rebuilds it. Every other workspace sees a no-op.

The update's tag fetches now accept moved tags (`git fetch upstream --tags --force`), and the worker guide says to report `stuck` rather than merge unrelated histories when no merge base exists.
