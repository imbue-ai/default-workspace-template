The `datalib` skill now installs datalib v0.47.0. Requests to Cloudflare-fronted sources (Claude, ChatGPT) and to Slack, GitHub and GitLab are decoded by datalib's own curl: the Imbue Studio gateway hands their compressed replies on as received, which fixes the Slack sync failing on a truncated reply. Notion sources bring everything under a root, databases' rows included, read a page as a document, and link inline databases rather than fetching them as pages. Search narrows by its keys before ranking, gives one fused answer with each document listed once, lands a hit on the message holding the search's words, and reaches eight more sources through `me` and `from:me`. Packages add sources, their renders and applets from a link or a gallery. In the Datalib app, every document has a title bar that stays put, a date chip can serve as T0 so every date reads as its distance from that instant, and charts draw their time axis from it. A Slack routine sync skips quiet channels, and a Google Drive sync of a few changed files takes seconds rather than minutes.
