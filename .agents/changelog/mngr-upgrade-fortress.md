The update apply no longer races the boot-time env-converge for the dpkg lock: after a successful apply, the post-success `env-converge upgrade` step first waits (bounded, 15 minutes) for the one-shot env-converge program to finish -- the apply's own services restart re-runs it, and a unit with new pins (this branch's Screen stack with Fortress v156) can keep it installing, and holding the dpkg lock, for minutes. Found by the upgrade end-to-end test, where the apply's `apt_full_upgrade` step failed with "Could not get lock /var/lib/dpkg/lock-frontend" while the Screen stack installed.
