Preview and UI-sketch frames now load their framed app on a shared workspace. The frame pointed its inner page at `<service name>.<workspace host>`, but a share routes only a service's registered `<name>-<rand>` origin label, so a visitor -- the owner included -- saw "Not shared with you" inside the frame (locally the forwarder's name fallback hid it). `preview_wrapper_server.py` takes `--apps-file` and reads the inner service's label from the app registry on each request, so a service registered again under a fresh label is followed, and it answers 503 when the service has no label. `serve_isolated_instance.py` passes the registry it registers into.
