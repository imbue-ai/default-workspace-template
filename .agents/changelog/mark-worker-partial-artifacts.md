A harden worker (crystallize / update / heal) can now declare a **milestone** before it reports `done`: a free-form, worker-chosen name pinned to a commit on its branch, written to `reports/milestones/<sha7>-<name>.md` with a required `## Tested` record of what was verified at that commit, pushed like any other report, after which the worker keeps working without stopping its turn. `create_worker.py await` returns such a file exactly as it returns `report.md` (`report.md` wins when both are present), `launch` refuses to start over an unconsumed milestone, and `launch-sync` ignores milestones while waiting and archives them once the terminal report lands.

On the lead side, `lead-proxy.md` and `harden-contention.md` describe the provisional merge of the pinned commit behind the existing pre-merge checks, the minimal go-live, the rollback rule (a reverted milestone must be reinstated or superseded before any later merge from that branch), and why the freshness rule composes: the merge-base advances to the milestone commit, so `done` brings only the remainder. `op-crystallize.md` has an app declare its milestone once its tests pass and a reconstructed skill only after its scenarios pass; `crystallize-creation` and `launch-task` list the report kind in their task templates and lead substitutions. Milestone names are never enumerated anywhere.
