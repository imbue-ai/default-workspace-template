The update-self flow now surfaces its own chat tab. The minds app sends the user into the workspace when it starts an update, and the conversation running it is what they are meant to land on -- but the workspace UI can only place a tab in front of a client that is connected when the chat appears, and from the app's machines list that is often nobody yet. Step 3 now runs a `surface-chat-tab` helper (from the target version's copy of the skill, so it reaches a workspace on its very first update) that keeps trying to open the tab until a client is there to show it, then stops for good; it is best-effort and never blocks the update.\n
