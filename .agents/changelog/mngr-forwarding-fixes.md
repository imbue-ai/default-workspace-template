- Skills and shared references are updated for the service-per-origin cutover: every registered service now owns its own browser origin (locally `http://<name>.<workspace-host>/`, e.g. `http://news.host-ab12.localhost:8421/`; share hostnames follow the same prefix rule) and nothing proxies or rewrites app traffic. `build-app` / `update-app` guidance drops the `/service/<name>/` path-prefix model ("the proxy handles prefixing" becomes "your app owns its origin; root-absolute URLs, WebSockets, cookies, service workers, and redirects just work"); in-container verification curls the registered `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/` backend directly; `cross-flow-gotchas.md` is rewritten down to the gotchas that still exist (loading-page symptom instead of the old "duplicated dockview tab bar" fall-through, DNS-safe names, no hardcoded `localhost:<port>` in browser-facing code, WS scheme on HTTPS shares, port collisions, loopback bind); `public-url.md` documents that share origins use the same service-name prefix rule on the share hostname.

- Registered service names are now validated as hostname labels: lowercase letters/digits with single hyphens, not `localhost`, and not starting with `host-` or `agent-` (reserved workspace coordinates). The Flask scaffolder rejects reserved-prefix names, and the `forward_port.py` CLI reference documents the validation.

- `preview_wrapper_server.py` no longer dodges the (deleted) HTML rewriter: the wrapper is registered as its own service and its inner iframe derives the previewed service's sibling origin from `location.host` in the page's JavaScript (the workspace host is not knowable server-side). `serve_isolated_instance.py up` now prints the preview's *service name* on stdout instead of a `/service/...` path, with `layout.py open` instructions on stderr.
