The `migrate-workspace` skill now leaves the old workspace's published share behind: its data copy excludes the share's identity files (`share.env`, `share_auth_label`, `share_gateway_signing_key`, `share_grants.toml` and its lock, `share_identities.json`, `share_tls/`, `agent_access/`) beside the backup and tunnel secrets, through a new `data-excludes` subcommand that owns the list. Copying them made the new workspace present the old one's hostname and certificate, with two machines claiming one share.

Chat recreation works for the chats a workspace has. `recreate-agents` selects the harness with `--type` (the old `--template claude` named a template the workspace does not define), binds each chat to the account it ran on the way the chat app does, with the chat app's `account` label, and recreates an agent that worked in a folder outside the checkout in that folder. `list-agents` reports each agent's harness, account and work folder, finds claude sessions under the account folders, and enumerates codex agents' rollouts. The skill gains an accounts step, inventories the directories outside the checkout and the gitignored files creations depend on, asks for `minds-workspaces-destroy` only at the close-out, and checks that the new workspace is backed up before the old one is stopped.
