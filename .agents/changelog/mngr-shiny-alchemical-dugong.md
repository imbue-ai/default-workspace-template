The workspace calls the desktop app Mind, following its rename from Minds.

The welcome message every new workspace opens with, the skills that name the app when they explain what went wrong or what to do next, the published-template README and its call-to-action, and the app catalog all say "Mind".

Identifiers are unchanged: the `minds-api` skill and the `minds-api-proxy` gateway route, the `minds-workspaces-*` permission scopes, and every `MINDS_*` variable keep their names.
