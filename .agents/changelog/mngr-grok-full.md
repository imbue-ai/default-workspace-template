The `use-ai-integration` skill's billing and credentialing reference names grok's account binding: a grok chat is bound to its account by `GROK_AUTH_PATH`, the account folder's `auth.json`. It also says what switching a chat's account does (a same-harness, same-lane switch rebinds in place, except on grok), where it used to say nothing rebinds a chat, and that codex, antigravity and pi chats are bound by a credential link in the agent's state dir rather than an env var.
