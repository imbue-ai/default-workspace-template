An app's crystallize pass now checks how the app works from a phone and when it is shared, right after the worker's first milestone, so the hardened core reaches the user first. The worker drives the app at phone sizes with touch (viewport tag, no sideways scroll, nothing reachable only on hover, tappable controls), fixes what it finds without changing the desktop layout the user confirmed, and commits a phone-sized browser test. It then reviews what a visiting person or agent can reach, against what an app can actually enforce: the app gains the request guard the built-in apps already have, a route that reveals a secret or credential answers visitors 403 on the server, content a visitor saves is rendered safely, changes made through the app record the requester's participant key, an app visitors can change keeps a bounded access log, and neither route descriptions nor the report claim more protection than that. The worker's `done` report gains `Phone:` and `Sharing:` lines, and anything on them it could not fix becomes a ticket. `web-frontend-testing.md` now points browser tests in the repo-root suite at its `module_browser` and `phone_context` fixtures rather than pytest-playwright's `page`, `context` and `browser`, which that conftest refuses, and a user-built app's own suite adds Playwright to its dev group and launches Fortress by its path.
