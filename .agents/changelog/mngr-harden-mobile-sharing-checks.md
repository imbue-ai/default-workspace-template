An app's crystallize pass now checks how the app works from a phone and when it is shared, right after the worker's first milestone, so the hardened core reaches the user first. The worker drives the app at phone sizes with touch (viewport tag, no sideways scroll, nothing reachable only on hover, tappable controls) and commits a phone-sized browser test. It then reviews every route in the app's API document for what a visitor or visiting agent could do with it: a route that hands a visitor a secret or credential becomes owner-only, changes are attributed by the visitor's `user_id`, and an app visitors can change keeps a bounded access log. Whether visitors may change, delete or spend is left to the user: the worker names those routes on a new `Sharing:` line of its `done` report, and the lead asks the user after go-live.
