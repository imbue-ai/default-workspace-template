The `build-app` scaffolder starts every new app with a route table (its page and shell modules declared schemaless, its health probe answering a model through `model_response`), and the skill's "The app describes its own API" section states the rule every route now lives by: an entry in the table, bodies read only through `parse_request_body`, JSON answered only through `model_response`, refusals through `error_response`, and a schemaless entry with its reason for a page, a file, a stream or a socket. The `update-app` skill's change step says a route change owes the table its models or its reason.
