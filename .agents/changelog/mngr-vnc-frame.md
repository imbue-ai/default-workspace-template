The `manage-desktop` skill names the Screen (the `screen` app, the workspace's one streamed Linux desktop) and points at its `screen` command for driving it, since the layout ops only place its window.

The skill's placement vocabulary and its `place` row name the `FULLSCREEN` state (`--state fullscreen`): the window fills the whole viewport, taskbar and title bar gone, and the user leaves it from the strip at the top edge.
