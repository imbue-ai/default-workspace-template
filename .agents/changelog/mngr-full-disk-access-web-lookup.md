- Online search and Full Disk Access work together. The first chat follows the `personalized-ideas` skill: the welcome's "Do you have any ideas?", then, after a no, one fixed offer with four buttons: **My Mac apps and the web** (the app-data request and the name search start in the same turn), **Only my Mac apps**, **Only the web**, and **I’ll tell you myself**. With both, when the search finds several people or none, the agent proposes at most three search details from the app data (an organization, a school, a field), one button each; only what the user picks is searched with, and raw app data never leaves the workspace. The confirm question has a button for every person it lists. `suggest-apps` is now the app-data part of `personalized-ideas`, and `read-app-data` no longer makes its first-chat offer on its own.
