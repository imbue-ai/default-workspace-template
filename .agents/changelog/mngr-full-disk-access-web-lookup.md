- Online search and Full Disk Access work together. The first chat follows the `personalized-ideas` skill: the welcome's "Do you have any ideas?", then, after a no, the fixed offer to search the web for the user's name. When the search finds several people or none, a **Use my Mac apps to narrow it down** button asks for app data and proposes at most three search details from it (an organization, a school, a field), one button each; only what the user picks is searched with, and raw app data never leaves the workspace. `suggest-apps` is now the app-data part of `personalized-ideas`, and `read-app-data` no longer makes its first-chat offer on its own.
